About InvestWise

InvestWise helps you explore educational information about investment options in the context of your goals, experience and preferences. Recommendations are deterministic profile-compatibility results—not forecasts, performance estimates or instructions to invest.

Optional Gemini tools are a separate educational layer. They do not determine or alter recommendation results. When enabled, Gemini receives your preference answers, selected investment metadata, and chat messages through the server; monetary amounts are not included.

InvestWise is an educational investment exploration tool. It does not provide personalized financial, tax, or legal advice and does not guarantee investment performance. Investments involve risk.

Consider speaking with a qualified financial professional about your circumstances before making financial decisions.
